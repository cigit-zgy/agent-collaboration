  remains under `/Users/wenv/Documents/skills/` and user discovery normally uses
  symlinks;
- `AGENTS.md` is explicitly Codex-oriented rather than claimed as an Anthropic
  compatibility standard.

The remaining work is local reconciliation and independent consistency verification.
