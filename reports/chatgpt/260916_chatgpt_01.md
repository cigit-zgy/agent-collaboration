# Align local reuse-first dependency policy

## Mission

Make the User-machine Codex policy reflect the current collaboration rule that implementation should prefer authoritative, actively maintained third-party libraries/tools over unnecessary custom reimplementation, while keeping stars/popularity as supporting rather than decisive evidence.

The local collaboration discovery/cache must resolve to policy content equivalent to the pinned collaboration revision above. The machine-wide Codex policy should carry only a compact reuse-first rule and route detailed selection semantics to collaboration owners.

## Authority and boundaries

Authoritative policy is the pinned collaboration revision:

- `references/collaboration/implementation.md`
- `references/project/prior-art.md`
- `SKILL.md`

Preserve project/user precedence and the existing Human in Loop policy exactly unless a direct conflict is discovered. Do not weaken semantic, destructive, security/permission, public/external, or explicit User checkpoints.

The machine-wide rule must not become a copied dependency-selection manual. It should state the principle and route to the pinned collaboration owners.

Third-party preference means:

- prefer official, standards-body, original-author, long-lived domain, or mature maintained implementations when fit is comparable;
- favor active maintenance/recent compatible releases, semantic/runtime fit, license/security posture, documentation/tests, and ecosystem stability;
- treat stars/forks/downloads only as supporting adoption signals, with no universal minimum star threshold;
- avoid heavy dependencies for trivial capabilities where a small direct implementation is clearer and lower-maintenance;
- do not reimplement a substantial mature capability without a concrete project-specific reason.
